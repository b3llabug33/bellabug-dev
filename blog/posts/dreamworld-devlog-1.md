I have been working on a new game called **dreamWorld** !! It is a cozy 3D game with a retro look, made in Unity. The plan is for it to become an online world where you make your own character, level up by doing quests, and decorate your own home base. It is still early, but it is finally starting to feel like a real game so I wanted to share some progress.

## make you

The first thing you see is the character creator, which I called "make you". Every piece of the character is generated with code: a Python script builds the character and all of her hair and clothes in Blender, then Unity puts it together. Right now there are 87 hair variations (style, bangs and length), 12 eye styles with sliders for size and spacing, movable eyebrows, noses and mouths, and an extras tab for blush, freckles and piercings.

![make you](blog/images/dreamworld-make-you.gif)

Clothes come in a bunch of colors and patterns like stripes, hearts and leopard print, and pants have high, mid and low rise options.

![outfits](blog/images/dreamworld-make-you.jpg)

The menus are styled like an old computer terminal, and the background is a glowing mushroom forest growing over a giant old computer. Most of the computer parts back there are from an asset pack I own, but everything on the character is my own.

## the first world

After you make your character you wake up in Mushroom Hollow, a little village in the woods. The town square and the paths are safe zones, but once you step off the path the monsters can get you.

![town square](blog/images/dreamworld-town.jpg)

The combat is inspired by Elden Ring. Rolling and attacking cost stamina and you can lock on to enemies. The first enemy is a little hopping mushroom called a shroomling. It squishes down before it jumps at you so you have time to roll out of the way.

![fighting a shroomling](blog/images/dreamworld-fight.gif)

Everyone starts at level 0. Defeating monsters gives you XP and coins, and every level gives you a point to put into health, stamina, magic, strength or luck.

![level up](blog/images/dreamworld-level-up.jpg)

When you die you respawn at your home base, and you lose any coins you did not save on your home computer.

![you woke up](blog/images/dreamworld-woke-up.jpg)

Further down the road I want players to be able to make and sell their own clothes and decor, and to play together online.
