This is my beetle Orion !!! She is a 2 month old rainbow stag beetle who I have raised since she was a tiny larvae. Rainbow stag beetles are nocturnal so I mainly see her running around her enclosure and climbing leaves at night, she really enjoys beetle jelly and bananas.
